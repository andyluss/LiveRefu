## 一、一句话钩子（用于胶囊图副标题 / 首行）

**中文**：向一个相信清洁能源的明天提交提案——代价是残渣。

**English**: *Submit your proposals to a future that believed in clean energy. The cost is residue.*

> 为什么是这句：它同时给出**题材**（复古未来/清洁能源乌托邦）、**你的动作**（提交提案）
> 与**核心张力**（代价）。这是全篇最重要的一句，其余文字都只是它的展开。

## 二、短描述（Steam 的 "About This Game" 前三行，也是搜索结果的摘要）

**中文**：

> 《未来残片》是一款**自走式防线 + 时代提案卡组**的 roguelite。
> 你把改造提案放到 8 个塔位上：每一次供电都更快、更强——也把一点代价留在图纸上。
> 残渣会扩散成**降级区**并持续伤害防线；清掉它，或者把代价挪到还没建东西的地方。

**English**:

> *Future Debris* is a **roguelite of self-running defense and era-proposal decks.**
> Place your proposals on eight tower slots. Every watt you generate comes faster and stronger — and leaves a little cost on the blueprint.
> Residue spreads into **Degradation Zones** that keep eating your line. Clean it, or move the cost somewhere you haven't built yet.

## 三、长描述（分小节，Steam 上按此排版）

### 中文

**你面对的不是敌人，是代价**

《未来残片》里没有"敌人"这个词。你在一座相信清洁能源的城市里提交改造提案：
每张提案都是一个塔、一台机器、一份规程。它们自动交战——**你的决策在"放什么、放哪里"**。
真正的对手是提案本身留下的东西：**残渣**。

**每 2 点残渣吃掉 1 点战力；每 4 点残渣向外扩张一个降级区；每个降级区都在持续扣你的基地。**

于是每一回合你都在做同一个选择题：
- **再放一张牌**（更强的输出，更快的腐烂）；
- 还是**花电力清理**（少一点输出，多一点活下去的时间）；
- 或者**把代价挪走**（有些势力真的能把残渣搬到还没建东西的地方）。

**四个势力，四种玩法，不只是四种颜色**

| 势力 | 姿态 | 通关条件 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 原子能委员会 | 净化：清理返还电力，清理量直接变成火力 | **守住**所有波次 |
| 郊区秩序 | 规避：残渣更少，干净的塔位还能多打输出 | **守住**所有波次 |
| 清运承包方 | 搬运：把残渣搬到空塔位，回收更划算 | 清空所有波次 |
| 辐射漫游者 | 燃料：残渣不再是惩罚，而是燃料 | 清空所有波次 |

> **"守成"与"清剿"是两套目标**，不是同一把尺子下的强弱：
> 前者要求每一波都达标，后者只要能清完最后一波。

**时代提案，可累积的规则**

每打赢一关，你会解锁新的**规则卡**——它们**累加、不会消失**，
因为你对这个纪元的理解在增长，而不是每次重来。
8 个手写关卡、60 张可构筑卡、章节化的战役推进与存档。

**特性一览**
- 8 个固定塔位、逐波配额、自动交战——**决策全在放置与卡组结构**
- 双资源：**电力**（本回合能做什么）与**残渣**（你为此付出的利息）
- **降级区**：会扩张、会持续伤害防线的区域，是本作真正的倒计时
- 4 个势力 × 2 套胜利条件 × 可累积的规则卡
- 一章完整战役（8 关，含 2 场首领战）+ 关卡选择与评级
- 确定性模拟：每一局的数值都可复现、可复盘

### English

**What you face isn't an enemy. It's a cost.**

There are no enemies in *Future Debris*. You submit renovation proposals to a city that believes in clean energy: each proposal is a tower, a machine, a protocol. They fight on their own — **your decisions are what you play and where you play it.** The real opponent is what the proposals leave behind: **residue.**

**Every 2 points of residue eats 1 point of might. Every 4 spreads a Degradation Zone. Every Zone keeps draining your line.**

So every turn is the same choice:
- **play another card** (more output, faster rot),
- **spend power to clean** (less output, more time),
- or **move the cost elsewhere** (some factions really can dump residue where nothing is built yet).

**Four factions, four ways to play — not four colors**

| Faction | Posture | Win condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Atomic Energy Commission | Purge: cleaning refunds power and becomes firepower | **Hold** every wave |
| Suburban Order | Avoid: less residue, and clean slots hit harder | **Hold** every wave |
| Haulage Contractors | Move: dump residue into empty slots; salvaging pays better | Clear every wave |
| Radiation Roamers | Feed: residue is fuel, not a penalty | Clear every wave |

> **"Hold" and "Clear" are two different objectives**, not a strength ranking on one scale: holding demands every wave succeed; clearing only demands the last one fall.

**Era proposals, cumulative rules**

Clear a level and you unlock new **rule cards** — they **accumulate and never expire**, because your understanding of the era grows rather than resetting. Eight hand-authored levels, 60 constructible cards, chapter-based campaign progression with saves.

**Feature list**
- Eight fixed tower slots, per-wave quotas, auto-resolving combat — **all decisions live in placement and deck shape**
- Two resources: **power** (what you can do this turn) and **residue** (the interest you pay)
- **Degradation Zones** that spread and drain — the real clock in this game
- 4 factions × 2 win conditions × cumulative rule cards
- One full campaign chapter (8 levels, 2 bosses) with level select and grading
- Deterministic simulation: every run is reproducible and reviewable
